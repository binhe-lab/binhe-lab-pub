My name is Abigail Zimmerman. I am a post-baccalaureate researcher in the He lab for the 2026-27 year. I am from the Chicago area, and I went to the University of Iowa to pursue my undergraduate degree in biology.

My research focuses on the HIL adhesin gene family in _Candida auris_. Adhesins play a role in fungal pathogenicity by allowing the fungi to colonize surfaces and the human microbiome. The HIL family has been identified as a family of adhesins enriched in _Candida auris_, but the specific functions of these adhesins in the context of causing disease are not yet known. My research will focus on elucidating these functions through the use of molecular cloning and functional tests.
