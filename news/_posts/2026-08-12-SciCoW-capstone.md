[Hubert Kicinski](https://binhe-lab/members/hubert-kicinski) and Bin hosted 12 students in our inaugoral Summer Scientific Coding Workshop (SciCoW) capstone event. Students spent a half day plus lunch in the Biology Building B20 room, where they learned to apply the Python skills they learned in the virtual workshop on real biology problems. Check out the 2026 archive on the [workshop website](https://scicow.binhe.org/archive/2026) for details!
