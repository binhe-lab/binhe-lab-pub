Congratulations to [Arya Sajadian](https://binhe-lab.org/members/arya-sajadian) and [Baylee Bruce](https://binhe-lab.org/members/baylee-bruce) on receiving competitive research awards! Arya received the **Linda and Rick Maxson Undergraduate Research Award** for 2026–2027. His project will characterize within-species variation in antifungal tolerance and resistance in the opportunistic yeast pathogen *C. glabrata*. Baylee received the Graduate College's [Ballard and Seashore Dissertation Fellowship](https://grad.uiowa.edu/funding/fellowships/ballard-seashore). Her research opens a new area of study on the evolution of pioneer factor activity among orthologous transcription factors in yeast. Well done, Arya and Baylee!
