Our undergraduate member [Noelle Michael](https://www.binhe-lab.org/members/noelle-michael) presented her research at the Summer Undergraduate Research Conference in the University of Iowa. Her work was on variation in growth at different pH among a panel of strains in the pathogenic yeast species, _C. glabrata_. Her graduate student mentor is [Jessica Miller](https://binhe-lab.org/members/jessica-miller). Congratulations on the great work!
